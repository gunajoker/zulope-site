Privacy Policy

1. Age Requirement

  • This app is intended for users aged 13 years and above only.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
  • If we become aware that a user under 13 has provided personal data, we will promptly delete that information.

2. Information We Collect

  • Name and phone number for account creation and authentication.
  • Booking details such as salon, service, stylist, and appointment time.
  • Location data to help show nearby salons and support map-based discovery.

3. Booking and Communication Data

  • Your phone number may be used for booking-related updates and communication through WhatsApp.
  • We do not send promotional or marketing SMS.

4. Reviews & Ratings

  • Customers may submit reviews and ratings for salons and stylists.
  • Reviews help improve transparency and service quality.

5. How We Use Your Data

  • Data is used for authentication, booking management, and app operations.
  • We do not sell or rent personal data for marketing purposes.
  • Data may be shared with service providers only as needed to run the platform, such as cloud hosting or map services.

6. Security & Retention

  • Reasonable security measures are used to protect your data from unauthorized access or misuse.
  • Data is retained only as long as necessary for platform operations, compliance, and moderation.
  • You may request deletion of your account and associated data through the delete account flow.

7. Your Rights

  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time.
  • To exercise these rights, use the delete account page.

Terms & Conditions

1. App Purpose

  • The app allows users to browse salons, view services, and make bookings.

2. Bookings & Availability

  • Appointments depend on salon availability.

3. Cancellation Policy

  • Cancellation rules vary by salon.

4. Communication

5. Reviews & Content Rules

  • Reviews must be genuine and based on real experiences.

6. Platform Disclaimer

  • We are not responsible for salon service quality or outcomes.

7. Account Suspension
